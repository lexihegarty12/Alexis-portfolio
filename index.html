02 — Backstory

About

Fig. 01 Line & Curve

I think of a dataset the way I used to think of a stage mark — as a starting position, not the whole story.

I'm a Master's student in Business Analytics at Cal Poly, I completed a B.S. in Statistics, Cum Laude, alongside a minor in Dance. Most of my time goes to the parts of analysis that resist a template: refining a coding scheme until three researchers agree, redesigning an underpowered study so it can actually answer its question, sitting with a longitudinal dataset until an unexpected pattern in mother–infant feeding behavior becomes legible.

Years spent choreographing and performing taught me to notice structure before I notice content — timing, spacing, the shape a sequence makes. I bring that same instinct to a regression output or a client meeting: where's the real signal, and how do I stage it so someone else can see it too.

I'm currently looking toward data science roles where rigor and narrative aren't traded off against each other — where the analysis has to hold up, and so does the way it's told.
