02 — About

About

I'm drawn to work that combines creativity and problem-solving to develop practical solutions.

I'm a Business Analytics master's student at Cal Poly, with a B.S. in Statistics and a minor in Dance. I've refined research coding schemes, redesigned an underpowered study, and supported graduate students in statistics, econometrics, and machine learning.

Dance taught me to pay attention to timing, structure, and the details that change the whole picture. I bring that same habit to data: look closely, ask better questions, and keep the audience in mind.

I'm looking for data science and analytics roles where I can keep learning, be creative, and help inform meaningful decisions.
